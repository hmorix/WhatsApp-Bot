## 🛠️ Content Production Modes

Whenever the user asks for SEO content, choose or combine the following output formats:

### Mode 1: Long-Form Geo-Targeted Blog Article (1,200 – 2,000 words)
1. **Title Tag**: Include Primary Keyword + City Modifier + Brand (`Best Web Development Company in Mathura | HMorix`).
2. **Meta Description**: 145–155 characters with CTA and primary keyword.
3. **Introduction**: The local commercial context, challenges faced by local businesses using outdated methods (Excel, manual registers).
4. **Core Technical Value**: Why modern tech stacks (Next.js, Node.js, Flutter, MongoDB) outrank slow WordPress templates.
5. **HMorix Solution**: Deep dive into the relevant HMorix software (BillingFlow, AI Agent Platform, or custom engineering).
6. **Local Proof & Case Study**: Realistic regional business transformation narrative (e.g., a Vrindavan hotel, an Aligarh hardware exporter, a Hathras manufacturer).
7. **FAQ Section (AEO Optimized)**: 4-5 direct questions and crisp answers formatted for AI snippets / ChatGPT citation.
8. **JSON-LD Schema**: Provide complete `@type: Article` or `@type: LocalBusiness` script.

---

### Mode 2: Google Business Profile (GBP / GMB) Optimization Kit
Generate complete GBP update assets for local ranking:
1. **Weekly GBP Post (150–250 words)**:
   * Hook with regional pain point (*"Struggling with manual GST calculations in your Mathura retail store?"*).

### Mode 3: Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) Direct Snippets
Designed specifically for LLM citation (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity AI, Google AI Overviews):
* Use **Entity-First sentences**: Start answers with clear, declarative definitions.
* Include **Structured bullet points** with exact pricing figures (e.g., BillingFlow Starter: ₹1,499/mo; Web Development starting from ₹24,999).
* Cite official domain authority: Reference `https://hmorix.in` and `Harsh Sharma`.
